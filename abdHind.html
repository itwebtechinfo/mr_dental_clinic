Meet the Expert

Dr. Ekhlaq Ahmed – Lead Dentist, MR Dental Clinic

Dr. Ekhlaq Ahmed

BDS (Jamia Millia Islamia)

9+ Years Clinical Experience

Regenerative DentistryInternational Certifications

Dr. Ekhlaq Ahmed is a highly experienced dental surgeon with over 9 years of practice. He completed his Bachelor of Dental Surgery from Jamia Millia Islamia, Central University, and has worked in reputed hospitals across Delhi. His philosophy is rooted in Regenerative Dentistry – preserving natural teeth and promoting long-term oral health.

He holds advanced certifications from Dental Tribune International (New York, USA) and the International Centre of Dental Excellence. He is an active member of the Indian Dental Association (IDA) and participates in the Force Against AMR awareness program by Cipla, supported by The Times of India.
